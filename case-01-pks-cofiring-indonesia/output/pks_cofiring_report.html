Palm kernel shell co-firing: life-cycle results

Brightway 2.5 model · ReCiPe 2016 (H) midpoint proxy · generated 2026-08-20 12:34 UTC

Functional unit. 1 MWh net electricity delivered to the Java-Bali grid.
System boundary. Cradle-to-grave for the fuel supply and conversion service: allocated palm kernel shell (PKS) upstream burden, drying and size reduction, transport (truck and barge), on-site handling, boiler combustion in a 300 MW sub-critical PLTU unit, FGD reagent supply, process water, and controlled ash (FABA) landfilling. Plant construction and decommissioning are excluded (cut-off), consistent with the original ISO 14067 study.
